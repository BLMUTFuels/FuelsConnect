The lightning caused Bear Wildfire started early in the fire season on June 8th 2021, in Bear Canyon and quickly exploded through Crandell Canyon and to the Price Canyon and Recreation area. After cresting the ridge and into Price Canyon and fuels treatments, the fire went from exhibiting aggressive fire behavior to a respectable backing fire in the open Ponderosa and mixed conifer stands.

 

Price Canyon Fuels Treatment Intersected: The Price Canyon Fuels Reduction Project is located on the Price Field Office in Carbon County, approximately 5 miles northwest of Helper. The prescribed burning consisted of 633 acres burned inside the 1,390 acre project boundary. Previous treatments on this project included 603 acres of thinning and piling completed in the summer of 2007 followed by a handpile burn in the fall of 2008.  Work on a shaded fuel break along the road leading to and around the campground began in 2002 and was completed in 2006. In 2011, a light underburn was implemented to reduce fuel loading leftover from the initial fuels reduction treatment and pile burning.

 

When the Bear Fire reached the Price Canyon Fuels Treatments it went from a stand destroying crown fire to a surface and backing fire.
