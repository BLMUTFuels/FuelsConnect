In August 2024, a power pole broke in high winds and sparked the Boulter fire. The fire spread rapidly north pushed by 40 mph winds, traveling nearly eight miles in just a few hours. As the Boulter fire progressed north, despite the high winds, the right flank was slowed and held in bullhog chips from the Lofgreen Fuels Treatments.

 

The primary goals of the fuels project were to:

  • Protect private property.
  • Maintain and improve sagebrush habitat for greater sage-grouse and other sagebrush dependent wildlife.
  • Promote ecosystem resiliency by maintaining robust understory vegetation.

 

Mastication and seeding treatments were implemented in a series of phases beginning in 2013 and finishing in 2017. A follow up herbicide treatment was completed in some areas in 2020 to control cheatgrass. In summary, the fuels treatments altered fuel composition and structure which moderated fire behavior and progression during extreme conditions.
