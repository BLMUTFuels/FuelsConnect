The Monument Fire was reported on July 22, 2023, south of Vernal in the Book Cliffs on Monument Ridge. The cause of the fire was determined to be lightning. The fire grew to 81 acres. The Monument Fire started in untreated lands and burned south into the 2014 Jack Trap Bullhog. The Monument Fire burned approximately 19 acres in the fuels treatment. Based on discussions with District suppression personnel who were assigned to the Monument Fire, the fuels treatments played a positive role in reducing fire behavior and slowing the forward progress of the fire. The following observations were made by a BLM Green River District Fuels Technician and Engine Boss managing the Monument Fire:

  • The fire started in the north and was pushed south by northerly winds.
  • The fire burned in the pinyon and juniper crowns in untreated areas. When the fire reached the bullhog treatments, it dropped to the ground, slowing fire growth and reducing the intensity of fire behavior.
  • Masticated fuels resulted in backing/flanking fire behavior rather than running fire. This reduction in fire behavior allowed for more effective use of ground resources on the fire, making the fire more manageable and easier to complete containment line and halting fire growth.
